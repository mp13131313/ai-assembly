## Verdict
- **Overall quality:** careful work, and mostly accurate. Every headline count I rechecked reproduces, either from the data or from the author's saved outputs. The two new defects are real and confirmed: the voice Step 1 cache-write race, and the Whanganui contract-note leak.
- **How far to trust it:** trust the counts, the cost arithmetic and both defects. Trust the per-field "Strong/Weak" verdicts only moderately. Read $67.40 as a lower bound on what Athens actually spent.
- **Single biggest issue:** the pooled 0.8% control hides large per-voice variance. The unloaded `smoke_test_chains` field alone reaches 3.5% for Whanganui and 2.4% for Scheherazade. As a result, `formative_experience` counts as "Strong" only because of Whanganui: 109 of its 141 hits are Whanganui's, and the other nine voices together score 0.55%, below the control. `unique_contribution` is rated "Strong per token" mainly because it is named in Step 2 weighings, but the Step 2 prompt explicitly asks for that naming. The document's own §2 discounts exactly this kind of evidence.

## Problems with the document
- **Inconsistent evidentiary standard.** §2 correctly says prompt-driven naming shows compliance, not that the field content mattered. §3 then uses exactly that evidence to rate `formative_experience` "Strong" and `unique_contribution` "Strong per token", and §6.1 builds on the second ("the strongest per-token field").
- **Pooled control.** The document reports no per-voice rates or controls. Voice-concentrated effects therefore pass as field-wide effects: formative_experience is essentially Whanganui, and the corpus rate is led by Scheherazade at 10.2%.
- **Cost coverage is unstated.** $67.40 and the $2.25-per-voice-night ablation basis cover only the calls that survived on disk. The cache-read pattern (Out-3b) shows that other calls ran.
- **Internal inconsistencies:** trim savings of $1–2 (Outcome) vs $0.6–1.4 (§6.3); ablation $25–40 (Outcome) vs $35–40 (§6.5, §7); "4 voices" vs 3; "13 of 30" vs 12; a duplicated sentence in the length row.
- **Reproducibility.** The scripts live only in a `/private/tmp` worktree scratchpad and are uncommitted. The token-size rendering (`assemble_system_prompt`) has no saved script. The numbers are meant to feed Task 1, C59 and Stage 4, so the scripts should be kept.
- **Stale context.** It omits the operator's Stage 4 cap of USD 10, recorded the same afternoon, even though it recommends a $35–40 run and Option B's "regen".
- **Rules:** compliant as far as I can see. The saved scripts import no API client, read athens-2026 and the worktree prompts, and write only JSON into their own scratchpad. No tracker edits. Claims are labelled.
- **Honesty of the method limits (§2):** good overall. The document names prevention fields, overdetermination, model prior, prompt naming, summarised traces, the missing counterfactual and the matching limits. It misses three: per-voice control variance, verbatim-vs-allusion for the corpus, and the fact that the cost figure is a lower bound.

2. **Did you check whether the 155 records cover every Athens Voice call?** Examples: the N3 16:18 first attempt, and the voice-nights with zero writes. *Why:* $67.40 is the ablation budget basis and the denominator for the 24% figure. *What would change:* if there were aborted runs, the real spend and the race's waste are both higher, and the cache fix gains priority.
3. **Setting aside the prompt-forced "named in N/30" counts, would you still rate `unique_contribution` and `formative_experience` "Strong"?** *Why:* §6.1 keeps `unique_contribution` as "the strongest per-token field". *What would change:* if the answer is no, it becomes Moderate or Weak, and the council-scoping rewrite loses priority.
4. **Did you check allusion rather than verbatim quotation for the corpus**, e.g. Plato's midwife and philosopher-king passages? *Why:* "44/81 never quoted" backs the Stage 4 idea of core-vs-occasional lists. *What would change:* if allusion is common, drop the corpus leg of §6.4.
5. **Which run would actually verify Option B, and which ablation variants would you keep under a $10–15 budget?** *Why:* the sentinel regen can't test a runtime routing change, and the Stage 4 cap is USD 10. *What would change:* either a smaller, fundable ablation, or Option B rescoped as a Voice Step 2 rerun.
